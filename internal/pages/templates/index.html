URLs

/<type>/<W>x<H>/?format=png gives an image of the type with the width W and the height H in pixels, from 32 to 32768 per side within the pixel limit of the server; format is png (the default) or jpeg. /<type>/ gives a 1024×1024 PNG image, and /<type>/info/ a page about the figure.
