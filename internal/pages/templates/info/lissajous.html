A Lissajous curve is the path of a point that swings left and right and up and down at the same time, with two different rhythms:

x=sin⁡(at+δ), y=sin⁡(bt)

The frequencies a and b set the number of loops, and the phase δ shifts one swing against the other. When a and b are whole numbers, the curve closes and repeats itself after

0≤t< 2πgcd⁡(a,b)

This is the only figure of Image89 that is not a fractal: it is a smooth line.

History

The American mathematician Nathaniel Bowditch studied these curves in 1815 with a pendulum that swings in two directions, so they are also called Bowditch curves. In 1857 the French physicist Jules Antoine Lissajous made sound visible with them: he fixed small mirrors to two tuning forks vibrating at right angles and bounced a beam of light off both onto a screen. The shape of the light figure showed the ratio of the two pitches.

Interesting facts

  • With a = b the curve is an ellipse: a circle for δ = π/2 and a straight line for δ = 0.
  • Engineers compared an unknown frequency with a known one on an oscilloscope: the ratio can be read from the loops of the figure.
  • When the ratio of the frequencies is not a fraction, the curve never closes and comes arbitrarily close to every point of its rectangle.
  • The logo of the Australian Broadcasting Corporation is a Lissajous figure: its designer Bill Kennard took it from an oscilloscope trace in the 1960s.
  • The Victorian harmonograph, a table with two pendulums and a pen, draws Lissajous curves that slowly spiral inwards as the pendulums lose energy.

How Image89 draws it

Image89 uses a = 3, b = 2 and δ = π/2 by default; the preset allows frequencies from 1 to 32. The curve is approximated by points joined into a closed red line on white. By default their number depends on the image, so that from one point to the next each coordinate changes by at most about two pixels:

N=⌈πR·max⁡(a,b)gcd⁡(a,b)⌉

where R is half the shorter side in pixels, from 256 to 65 536 points. The two-pixel bound does not apply when the calculated count exceeds 65 536. The line is 0.4% of the shorter side wide, but at least one pixel, and the curve is fitted without distortion with a margin of 5% of the shorter side. The preset lives in the [types.lissajous] section of the config.
